**In one line —** we no longer draw the screen in advance. FloUI designs a single screen for itself, around a question nobody could have predicted.

## A screen drawn the moment you ask

Ask "show me this quarter's revenue trend" and FloUI combines the search experience, RAG references, and structured data, laying out a RAG summary, charts, and KPI cards in real time.

## It evolves with how people use it

In the flow of asking, checking, and pinning, a screen that has proven itself is pinned as an official screen and carries over to the next user. It is built on XGEN's own responsive UI library, so it is not tied to an external standard.
