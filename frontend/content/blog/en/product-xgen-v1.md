The **XGEN Agentic AI Platform** covers what an Enterprise AI platform has to cover — building AI agents, integrating with existing systems, GraphRAG-based knowledge management, and AI governance — and it passed quality testing against the international standards ISO/IEC 25023 and ISO/IEC 25051 in GS certification at TTA, Korea's accredited testing body.

[An earlier post](/en/blog/gs-certification-grade1) covered how the XGEN Agentic AI Platform reached Grade 1 GS certification, and [how we worked through the improvements found during testing](/en/blog/series/gs-cert).

This time we want to tell a slightly different story.

GS certification is not a procedure where someone installs the product and looks at it. The Telecommunications Technology Association (TTA), an accredited testing body, verifies a product's functionality and quality directly, against the international standards ISO/IEC 25023 and ISO/IEC 25051.

So what did TTA actually check in XGEN?

This post walks through the core capabilities of the XGEN Agentic AI Platform, organized around what the testing verified.

---

## A platform for designing and operating AI agents yourself

Enterprise AI is no longer a chatbot.

**AI agents** — which understand the work, connect to several systems, and automate what repeats — are settling in as a new way of working.

XGEN lets business users, not only developers, design and operate those agents themselves.

TTA's testing confirmed that **42 core agent nodes** work as intended.

The main capabilities:

* Agent workflow design
* MCP and agent integration
* API and document-processing nodes
* Conditional branching and iteration
* Prompt template management
* Agent template management
* Testing and deployment

Agents used repeatedly can be saved as templates and reused across the organization, which is how AI services spread and become standardized.

---

## Enterprise AI that connects to the systems already in place

For enterprise AI to carry out real work, it has to integrate with what is already running — ERP, groupware, databases.

XGEN provides the following so those systems can be connected and operated reliably:

* REST API registration and management
* JSON-based API definition
* API library management
* Database connection management
* Credential management
* Connection testing

Testing a connection before registering it means integration errors that would otherwise surface in operation get caught in advance.

---

## A knowledge platform that turns enterprise documents into AI knowledge

The quality of a generative AI's answers depends on how accurately it draws on the knowledge inside the enterprise.

XGEN manages documents as knowledge collections and supports building RAG-based AI services on top of them.

Supported document formats:

* PDF
* Word
* Excel
* PowerPoint
* TXT
* HWP / HWPX

Beyond uploading, chunk settings and change history are managed alongside, which improves traceability and makes the collections easier to operate.

---

## Advanced knowledge search on GraphRAG

In enterprise work, keyword search alone is often not enough.

XGEN uses a knowledge graph and [GraphRAG](/en/blog/series/ontology) to analyze the relationships and meaning between documents.

Testing confirmed the following work as intended:

* Ontology build
* Knowledge graph generation
* GraphRAG question answering
* Graph browsing
* Inspecting how an answer was produced

Together they give more accurate knowledge search, in an environment where the basis for an answer can be checked.

---

## Governance for operating Enterprise AI

For an AI platform, operating it matters more than building it.

XGEN ships the operations and administration capabilities an enterprise environment needs:

* User and permission management
* Menu access control
* LLM management
* Embedding model management
* Reranker management
* Vector database management
* Agent pre-validation
* System log management
* Server resource monitoring
* User feedback management

Models are managed independently, so commercial LLMs and on-premise sLLMs can both be applied flexibly.

## Software quality, verified in GS certification

TTA tested XGEN against the international standards across nine quality characteristics:

* Functional suitability
* Performance efficiency
* Compatibility
* Usability
* Reliability
* Security
* Maintainability
* Portability
* General requirements

Every improvement identified during testing went through a fix and a regression test, and the final state was confirmed at **zero defects**.

That means XGEN meets the software quality bar an enterprise environment demands.

> Note: the security evaluation in GS certification is a software-quality test. It does not replace an information-security certification or a source-code security assessment.
