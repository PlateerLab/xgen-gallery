**Home-shopping retailer L**, a distribution and remote-sales business, built an **automated insight reporter** on the XGEN Agentic AI Platform. Reports that had been written by hand, over and over, are now generated automatically — and decisions in product sourcing and planning move faster.

## Trends changed daily; the report was written by hand

Retail and e-commerce move from one day to the next with the season and whatever is in the news. But L's market report was still produced the old way: a person going channel to channel, gathering data, writing it up. Three burdens followed.

- A **limit on how fast** a fast-moving market trend could be recognized
- **Difficulty getting trend-based insight in time** for product sourcing and planning
- **Lost productivity** from writing the same kind of report by hand

By the time a report was finished, the market had often moved to its next phase. The problem was not a lack of data; it was the time it took a person to connect scattered data by hand.

## Internal and external data through one lens

We designed the insight reporter around automating **the judgment, not the data collection**.

- **Combined analysis** — external data from the three home-shopping networks, the company's own mall, and Naver Shopping, analyzed together with product inventory and media data
- **Competitive comparison** — internal sales data linked to external market data to draw out the competitive picture and the insight in it
- **Automated reports** — the trends and insights that come out are generated as a report in the company's own format and distributed

Ask in a single sentence — "pull together this week's top 10 search terms, the fastest-rising keywords, and news-based insight" — and out comes a deep-dive report on the latest data, with a market summary and ranking tables. Instead of gathering data, the analyst spends the time **reviewing the conclusions and deciding**.

## It did not cut reports; it made decisions faster

The effect went past simple time savings.

- Automating a market report that had been manual raised **operational efficiency**
- **Product sourcing and planning decisions** could now rest on live trend and competitor data
- Combined insight from external and internal data strengthened **competitiveness in the market**

The real value of report automation was not "you no longer have to write reports." It was "you can decide faster, and more often."
