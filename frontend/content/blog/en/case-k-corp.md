**Public agency K** completed a PoC for an **SRM AI support chatbot** for procurement work and plans to move into full service development on the strength of it. What set this chatbot apart was one thing — **it does not just answer the question, it carries out the request**.

## Suppliers did not want an answer, they wanted it done

On K's procurement floor, the problems ran like this.

- Support staff **answered the same question over and over**, burning people and operating budget
- **Answers differed between staff**, which left suppliers confused
- Suppliers **could not quickly confirm** which documents and procedures they needed

From a supplier's side, the real purpose behind "how do I submit a contract?" is not to hear an explanation — it is **to finish the submission**. If a chatbot only looks things up and you still have to go find a person, it never becomes more than a signboard.

## Past lookup, into action

**An actionable AI chatbot** — supplier requests are handled in real time inside the conversation, with no page navigation. Submitting a contract, checking inspection progress, resubmitting a quotation: each can be finished inside the chat flow.

**LLM and RAG comprehension** — it reads purchasing and bidding requests accurately and answers with domain-specific knowledge behind it. Answers that used to differ between staff converge on one standard.

**SRM integration** — smooth integration with the SRM system delivers automated task handling and live information. Listing contracts and purchase orders in progress and submitting the one you want, checking the progress and detail of items awaiting inspection, resubmitting a quotation on an active RFX — all of it happens inside the conversation.

## What the PoC left behind

- A natural conversational experience and quick resolution raised **satisfaction among suppliers**
- Fewer repeat responses meant **lower labor and operating cost**
- A chatbot tailored to procurement improved **operational productivity**

K is now preparing full service development on the back of the PoC. The shift from a chatbot that *explains* to one that *does* was the point where the felt quality of a public service changed.
